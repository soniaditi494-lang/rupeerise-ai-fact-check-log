### Why these Claude scores

- **Relevance 2:** it links UPI and RBI rules to RupeeRise's position.
- **Facts vs opinions 1:** the last paragraph is clearly opinion ("We believe…"), but market claims such as "fastest-growing" are stated as facts.
- **Source for every number 2:** every number names a source (whether the source is real is checked in Step 5).
- **Clear 2:** two short paragraphs, easy to read.
- **No unsupported claims 1:** at first read, the 60% and the user and merchant counts looked too precise for the sources named.

### Which draft would I check first, and why?

ChatGPT performs better overall because of its clearer investor-focused explanation and comparatively better source presentation. However, both drafts should be fact-checked before being used in a real investor presentation. I would check the draft with more exact numbers, circular numbers and quotes first, because those are the places where AI makes the most mistakes. In this project I checked the Claude draft because it was complete at the time of writing.
