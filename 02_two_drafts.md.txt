**India's Digital Lending and UPI Market: Investor Brief for RupeeRise**

India is the world's largest real-time payments market, and digital lending is its fastest-growing credit channel. UPI processed 20.0 billion transactions worth ₹24.9 lakh crore in August 2025 (Source: NPCI), roughly 34% more than a year earlier. UPI now has over 350 million active users and more than 50 million merchants (Source: NPCI). India's digital lending market is projected to reach $1.3 trillion by 2030, growing at about 22% a year (Source: BCG–Google report). Digital lending already accounts for around 60% of all new retail loans by value (Source: RBI Financial Stability Report).

The regulatory framework is clear and borrower-focused. First, the RBI Guidelines on Digital Lending (circular RBI/2022-23/111, dated 2 September 2022) require loans to be disbursed directly into the borrower's bank account, with no pass-through by third parties. Second, the RBI (Digital Lending) Directions, 2025 (issued 8 May 2025) require a Key Fact Statement before loan execution and a cooling-off period of at least three days during which borrowers can exit a loan without penalty (Source: RBI).

## Draft 2: ChatGPT

**Tool:** ChatGPT · **Date:** *[25 November 2026]*

INVESTOR BRIEF
RupeeRise – India's Digital Lending & UPI Opportunity
Location: Bengaluru, India
Industry: Financial Technology (FinTech)
Market Overview
India's digital finance ecosystem presents a significant investment opportunity. Digital lending sanctions have increased from ₹0.15 trillion to ₹2.2 trillion over the past five years, reflecting rapid adoption of technology-driven credit services (Source: Redseer, 2026). Furthermore, mass-market digital lending is projected to reach ₹4.1 trillion by FY2030, growing at a 48% CAGR (Source: 1Lattice Report, 2026).
UPI is strengthening this opportunity. According to NPCI data, India recorded 24,161.69 crore UPI transactions worth ₹314 lakh crore in FY2025–26, representing 30% year-on-year volume growth. UPI had also onboarded 55.49 crore users by June 2026 (Source: Government of India, PIB, July 2026).
Regulatory Environment
RupeeRise will operate within RBI's regulatory framework:
1. RBI Digital Lending Directions, 2025 (RBI/2025-26/36): Requires regulated lenders to assess borrower creditworthiness and provide transparent loan disclosures.
2. RBI Key Facts Statement Circular (DOR.STR.REC.13/13.03.00/2024-25, April 15, 2024): Requires lenders to disclose key loan information, including the Annual Percentage Rate (APR), to borrowers.
Industry Perspective
Dilip Asbe, MD & CEO of NPCI, stated:
"Digital payments are now integral to life—I don't think we're going back to cash."
Investment Outlook
RupeeRise aims to leverage India's expanding digital payment infrastructure, responsible AI-based credit assessment and seamless loan processing. By prioritising transparency, customer trust and responsible lending, the platform can address underserved credit demand and build a scalable fintech business.
Investment Proposition: A technology-driven lending opportunity supported by India's rapidly expanding digital financial infrastructure.
