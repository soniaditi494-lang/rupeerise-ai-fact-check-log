# Step 1: The RupeeRise Case Study

**Project:** RupeeRise AI Fact-Check Log  
**Course:** Prompt Engineering, AI & Machine Learning | BBA (FinTech), Chitkara Business School  
**Tools:** Claude (Anthropic); ChatGPT and Perplexity: *[to be completed by the author, see notes]*  

## Background

**RupeeRise** is a fictional digital lending app in Bengaluru that gives small personal loans to young salaried workers. Its founder is meeting investors next week and asked an AI tool to write a one-page brief on India's digital lending and UPI market.

## The question

The founder asks me, as a Business Analyst:

> **"The brief reads really well. Can I send it to investors as it is?"**

My job is to find out, claim by claim.

## Sources that count as proof

| Topic | Official source |
|---|---|
| UPI and payments data | NPCI website (npci.org.in) |
| Lending rules and circulars | RBI website (rbi.org.in) |
| Government announcements | PIB (pib.gov.in) |
| Company figures | The company's own annual report or official press release |
| **Not good enough** | Blogs, social media, news without a source, or another AI tool's answer |

## Verdict labels used in this project

| Verdict | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Correct** | Matches the official source |
| **Outdated** | Was true earlier but has changed |
| **Distorted** | A real fact, but with the wrong number, date or meaning |
| **Fabricated** | The source, circular, quote or number does not exist |
| **Unsupported** | Cannot be found in any reliable source |

## My approach

1. Get the same investor brief from two AI tools and compare them.
2. List every checkable claim in one draft and ask the AI to rate its own confidence.
3. Verify each claim at an official source and record a verdict with a link.
4. Check the sources an AI search tool cites.
5. Write a corrected brief and a guide to using AI safely in finance.
