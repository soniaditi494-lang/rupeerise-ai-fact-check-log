## Part B: Guide to using AI safely in finance work

| Rule | Evidence from my log |
|---|---|
| Never send an AI number to investors without opening the official source. | C01–C03: AI said 20.0 billion, ₹24.9 lakh crore, about 34%; the RBI page shows 20.01 billion, ₹24.85 lakh crore, +33.7%. These were right, but C04–C08 in the same brief were not, so only checking each claim tells you which is which. |
| Treat a precise statistic with a named source as unverified until you find it in that source. | C08: AI said 60% of new retail loans (RBI Financial Stability Report); I could not find it in RBI publications. |
| Check who really published a figure. | C06 and C07: AI credited $1.3 trillion and 22% to a "BCG–Google report"; the figures trace to Inc42's 2022 report. |
| Check whether a circular is still in force. | C09: AI cited RBI/2022-23/111 as the rule; RBI shows it repealed by the 2025 Directions. |
| Check exact thresholds and numbers inside rules. | C12: AI said a cooling-off period of at least three days; para 10 says not less than one day. |
| Do not treat the AI's confidence rating as proof. | C09 was rated High but is outdated; C04 to C07 and C12 were rated Medium and were not Correct. |
| Do not use media reports or blogs as proof of a number; trace it to NPCI, RBI or PIB. | C06: the $1.3 trillion figure is only in a media report, not at RBI or PIB; October 2026 UPI figures are marked [not verified] until opened at NPCI. |
| Date every statistic and look for the latest official data. | C01: August 2025 data was correct but 14 months old by November 2026; FY2025-26 data (PIB) and newer months exist. |
| Do not ask AI to invent a quotation; if it declines, leave the quote out. | The Claude draft refused to invent a quote and left a placeholder; the other claims show why an unverifiable quote would be a risk. |
| Keep a human sign-off before anything reaches investors. | 7 of 12 claims were not Correct even though the draft read fluently. |
